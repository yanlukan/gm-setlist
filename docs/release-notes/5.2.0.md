# PlayBook 5.2.0: the chord shapes are back in Practice

Since the rework (5.0.0) Practice showed the chart alone, and a chord's shapes opened only when you tapped it. The player asked where the shapes had gone.

- **A row of shapes under the chart.** Practice shows one picture for every chord in the song, in the order the chords first come, each in the shape used in this song: your own pick, or the recommended one. Under each picture is where it sits on the neck (Open, 7th fret).
- **Tap a picture for all the shapes of that chord.** The same sheet opens as from a tap on the chart, and a shape you pick there is the one the row then shows.
- **A long song scrolls the row sideways.** The chart keeps its height; a picture cut at the right edge says there are more.
- **Put it away when you want the chart bigger.** The thin bar above the row hides it and brings it back, and the app remembers. With the row showing the chart is a little smaller, and the longest charts scroll sooner on an iPad on its side.
- **Where it does not show.** Stage has no shapes, as before. The row makes way for the chord keyboard while you edit, is not shown in the keyboard view, and is left out on a phone held on its side, where there is no height for it.
