# PlayBook 5.2.6: the other songs checked against the recordings

After Roxanne, Everything She Wants and Outside, the player asked for every other song to be checked the same way: the chart laid bar by bar on the recording's separated bass, guitar, keys and voice. Fourteen songs match. Four charts changed.

## Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go

- **Link (new part), after the first and the second chorus.** The chart repeated the four opening bars of C there. The recording has the organ on other chords: `| F C7 | C7 | F C7 | C7 C |`.
- **Verse.** Was C then Dm in every bar. On the recording bars 1 and 3 go C, Dm on the third beat, C on the fourth; bar 2 stays on C; bar 4 is Dm.
- **Chorus.** Every second bar is Dm for two beats and C for the other two, the last bar included. The chart had a whole bar of Dm, and C for the last bar.
- **Instrumental (new part), before the Bridge.** The band plays the verse once with no singing, ending on C. Its last bar is half as long, so the Bridge comes in two beats early. The chart had a full fourth verse there.
- The song order is now Intro, Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Link, Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Link, Instrumental, Bridge, Chorus three times.

## Club Tropicana

- **The groove.** The chart had `| F#m7 | B7 |`. On the recording the second bar is B for two beats and then E for two (bass B, D#, E, D#; the keys on E). Written `| F#m7 | B E |` in the second intro, the verses, the instrumental, the last verse and the outro. In the band's key that is Em7, then A and D.
- Under the bass solo the keys stay on B: `| F#m7 | B |`.
- B7 is written B: neither the keys nor the guitar play the seventh there.
- The bar that ends each verse is B, B/D# on the second and third beats, E on the fourth.

## Papa Was a Rolling Stone

- **The opening riff, counted from the voice on the recording.** The two-bar riff plays 3 times before the voice, 7 times under the first Killer verse, 6 times on its own (the voice picks up in its last bar) and 4 times under the second verse: 40 bars before the first chord. The book's count, which the chart had, is 9, 16, 8 and 8 bars.
- Everything after that matches: the Killer chords six times round, the trumpet solo, the break, 40 bars of Bbm7 and the ending.

## Somebody to Love

- **Outro.** Same chords, placed as the recording plays them in the ending: G and D/F# share the first bar; Em takes half of the second, then C and D. The intro keeps the book's rhythm.

## Songs that match

I'm Your Man, Amazing, Father Figure, Freedom! '90, Jesus to a Child, A Different Corner, Too Funky, Careless Whisper, I Can't Make You Love Me, Kissing a Fool, Faith, Fastlove, Last Christmas and Waiting (Reprise): every part is on the chart and in the recording's order. The openings the charts leave out are the ones their notes already name (Faith's organ, the keyboard openings of Amazing and Father Figure, four quiet bars before Freedom! '90 and Fastlove, Club Tropicana's sound effects).

## Left as they are

- **Everything She Wants, third bar of the verse groove.** The bass plays B under the book's Esus24. The book's shape still fits over it, so the chart keeps the book's chord and shape.
- **Fastlove, third bar of the chorus.** Two passing chords on the last two beats over the Am7; not written.
- **Wake Me Up's pre-chorus and Somebody to Love's held D before the stop** read close to the chart and stay.

A chart the player edited on a device stays as saved; "back to original" brings the new version.
