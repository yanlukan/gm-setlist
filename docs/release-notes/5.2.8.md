# PlayBook 5.2.8: Kissing a Fool's parts are named as they are played

The player said the parts of Kissing a Fool were named wrong. They were: the songbook prints no part names, and the names on the chart were the chart writer's own.

- **There is no Chorus any more.** The 16 bars that were called Chorus run on the verse's chord loop twice more, and the title is only sung at the very end of the song. They are now part of the **Verse**: 24 bars and one link bar, the same 8-bar loop three times with a different tune each time round.
- **Bridge 2 is now Middle 8.** It is different music from the Bridge (other chords, other tune, sung higher), not the Bridge again. It has the bridge's colour.
- The song order reads Intro, Verse, Bridge, Verse, Middle 8, Last verse, Outro.
- No chord and no bar changed, and the parts stay in the same places on the recording.
- Everything She Wants' Middle takes the bridge colour too, as a middle part is a bridge by another name.

Checked on the recording's voice: the two halves of the old Chorus are sung with the same words both times round on the verse's chords, and the Bridge and the Middle 8 share neither tune nor words. The player chose the two names.

A song order the player saved for this song with the old names keeps them until "back to original".
