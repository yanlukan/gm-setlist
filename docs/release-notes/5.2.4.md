# PlayBook 5.2.4: Everything She Wants gets its middle part

The player: "everything she want is missing a part". It was: the sung middle part after the third chorus, 4:16 to 5:23 on the player's recording (the 12-inch version). The chart's note had called it 32 bars the book doesn't have and left them out.

- **New part, "Middle"**, 16 bars, played twice, between the third chorus and the Instrumental:
  - F#m, F#m, C#m7, C#7, twice
  - Bm7, C#m7, F#m, B7
  - D, D, G#m7, C#7
- **The song order** now reads: Bridge, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Middle twice, Instrumental, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Outro.
- The chords are read from the recording, bar by bar: the bass note on each beat, the notes of the keyboards, and the chord model on the instruments without voice and drums. Both times through read the same. The book has no boxes for these chords, so their shapes are the app's recommended ones.
- The chart now comes to the length of the recording with nothing left out.

**Outside** was measured the same way after the player thought a part was missing there too. The chart has every part of the recording, in the same order and length: 16 bars of intro, two verses, chorus, 8 bars of groove, verse, the 8-bar break, chorus, 8 bars of groove, and the fading outro. Nothing was changed.
