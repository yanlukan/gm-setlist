# PlayBook 5.2.21: chords on their beats in five more songs

After Roxanne, every bar with two or more chords in the other 20 songs was laid on the separated instruments an eighth at a time: the bass note first, then keys and guitar. A bar written without beats draws its chords evenly across the bar; where the recording plays them on other beats, the bar now shows that.

## What you see

- **Careless Whisper, bar 2 of the loop (Gm7 Am7), every part.** Gm7 for three beats; the Am7 is a passing chord on beat 4 (it was drawn at the half bar).
- **Everything She Wants, Bm C#aug** (Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Outro). Bm for three beats, C#aug on beat 4 (was the half bar).
- **Somebody to Love, the three-chord bars** (Intro, Verse, Solo): G D/F# Em, G A D, G A7 D. One beat, one beat, then the last chord for two beats (they were drawn as equal thirds).
- **Faith, F# then the stop before the chorus.** F# for three beats, silence on beat 4 (was the half bar).
- **Faith, third bar of the Verse and the Solo.** The chart had E then B in the first line and E alone in the second. On the recording both are E for the whole bar; the B comes with the next bar. The two lines are now the same and written once, twice round.
- **Club Tropicana, last bar of the Chorus.** D#m7 for three beats, the slide down on beat 4 (it was drawn as equal thirds). The Dm7 is clear on beat 4. The C#m7 after it is kept from the transcription on the last eighth; the bass does not play it on this recording.

No bar count changes in any song.

## Read and left as drawn

Chords that come in an eighth early (a push) stay on the beat they push into: Fastlove's Bridge, Wake Me Up's verse, I Can't Make You Love Me. Father Figure, I'm Your Man, Kissing a Fool, the rest of Club Tropicana and the two-chord bars of Somebody to Love read as drawn.

## Not settled

- **Freedom! '90, F/C C in the verse; A Different Corner, Gb/Db Gb; Too Funky, the chords over the D riff; Outside, F/G G7.** The two chords share most of their notes, or sit over a riff, and the reading does not say clearly where the change falls. Left as they were.
- **Somebody to Love, last bar of the Chorus (G D/F# Em C).** The two rounds read differently. Left a beat each.
- **Papa Was a Rolling Stone, Bbm Gb and Fm Ab.** The bass plays the riff all through, so it does not show where the chord changes. Not read.
