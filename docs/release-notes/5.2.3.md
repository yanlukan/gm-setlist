# PlayBook 5.2.3: Roxanne re-read from the recording, bar by bar

The player asked for the rest of Roxanne to be read from the recording, after the book had been wrong twice. Every bar was read three ways: the bass note on each beat, the notes of the piano, guitar and the other instruments, and a chord model run on the instruments without voice and drums.

**One bar changed.**

- **The last bar of Verse 2.** The book holds Bm7. On the recording the Bm7 rings on for a beat, then the bass and piano walk up a beat each: E/G#, A/C#, F#m, into the Chorus.

**The rest holds.** Gmaj7, F#m7, Em7, the chorus's Em9, the Pre-Chorus, G13 and the Ending read on the recording as the chart has them. Four places are a matter of colour, and the chart keeps its chord:

- **F#m in the sung verses.** The piano plays F#m over the bass's A, as in the intro. Under the voice another instrument adds a D, which makes the book's D/A. F#m sits inside that chord.
- **G/A.** The piano sometimes adds a C#, which makes it A9. G/A fits both.
- **B11 at the end of the Pre-Chorus.** The piano adds a D there, which makes it Bm11. B11 has no third, so it fits. Everywhere else the B11 is as written.
- **A in the Interlude.** The same notes as in the Intro, but the bass plays F# under them, which makes it F#m7.

Two things the chart writes for the guitar, not for the band: at each "N.C. E Bm7" the bass and piano keep playing, and the guitar's stab comes on beat 2; the N.C. that ends the first chorus is a quiet piano chord far from the key, where the guitar stays silent.

Not settled by this reading: whether the second chord in the Outro's first bar is the book's C9 or a C6/9. The recording shows C, E, A and D clearly and no B flat.
