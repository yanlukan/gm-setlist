# PlayBook 5.2.7: the GX-10 hints follow the new sounds

On 2026-11-24 the 21 new sounds went into the GX-10's gig banks 01–07, under the song names. The chart's GX-10 line (the sound's name, what CTL1 does, the CTL1 tags on the parts) still described the October sounds. It now describes what is in the unit.

## What changed

- **CTL1 has a job in 20 of the 21 songs now** (all but Waiting), and the header says what it does. New: Amazing (doubler), I'm Your Man (drive), I Can't Make You Love Me (lift), Too Funky (phaser), Club Tropicana (phaser), Father Figure (nylon solo), Everything She Wants (scratch), Jesus to a Child (solo voice), Wake Me Up (drive), Roxanne (delay), Kissing a Fool (delay), Careless Whisper (nylon), Last Christmas (bells).
- **Papa Was a Rolling Stone.** Light off is one strong sound for the Bb chords and the bass line; CTL1 is the wah; the toe switch does nothing. The old tag that switched CTL1 on for the whole Papa part is gone: where the wah goes is the player's choice.
- **Freedom! '90.** CTL1 brings the electric guitar forward (it was a touch wah). Same parts as before.
- **Outside.** The pedal is the wah and is rocked by foot; the toe switch turns the wah off and makes the pedal a volume pedal.
- **CTL1 tags on the parts** are given only where the place is settled: I'm Your Man, A Different Corner, Fastlove, Too Funky, Club Tropicana, Faith, Everything She Wants, Outside, Freedom! '90, Roxanne, Careless Whisper, Somebody to Love, Last Christmas. Where the place is only a suggestion from the record (Amazing, Kissing a Fool, Wake Me Up) the note says so and no part is tagged.

## Not checked

None of the new sounds has been played at an amp yet, and the CTL1 places for the newly tagged songs have not been tried by the player.
