# PlayBook 5.1.0: the Cryer Arts Theatre running order

The built-in GM Tribute order is now the band's list of 2026-11-22: the same 21 songs, in two parts.

Part 1: 1 Waiting (Reprise), 2 Amazing, 3 I'm Your Man, 4 Papa Was a Rolling Stone (the Killer / Papa medley, one chart), 5 I Can't Make You Love Me, 6 A Different Corner, 7 Fastlove, 8 Too Funky, 9 Club Tropicana, 10 Father Figure.

Part 2: 11 Faith, 12 Everything She Wants, 13 Outside, 14 Jesus to a Child, 15 Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go, 16 Freedom! '90, 17 Roxanne, 18 Kissing a Fool, 19 Careless Whisper, 20 Somebody to Love, 21 Last Christmas.

- **Your saved setlist does not change by itself.** A device that has used the app keeps its own order. To get the new one: update the app, open the GM Tribute setlist, then More, "Restore the GM Tribute order". A restore point is saved first. Do it in each copy (home-screen app and Safari keep separate data). It rebuilds whichever setlist is open, so open the right one first.
- **A new install** opens in the new order, in a setlist named "GM Tribute — Cryer Arts".
- **No interval mark.** A setlist is a plain list of songs; the app has nothing to mark a break with. The interval is after song 10, Father Figure: the Songs button reads 10/21 there.
- **GX-10 memories are not in this order yet.** The memory shown for each song (U01-1 to U07-3) is still where the unit has its sound, laid out in the September order, so UP/DOWN does not step through this set. The Next button still names the memory of the next song. This changes when the unit is laid out again and the sounds file is copied from the gx10 branch.
- Charts, keys, cues and notes are unchanged; none of them referred to a song's place in the set.
