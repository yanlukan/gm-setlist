# PlayBook 5.1.2: the new running order shows up by itself

5.1.0 put the Cryer Arts Theatre order in the app, but a device that had been used before kept showing its old setlist, and the new order sat behind More, "Restore the GM Tribute order". The player could not see it.

- **The new order arrives as a setlist of its own.** The first time the app opens after this update, a setlist named "GM Tribute — Cryer Arts" is added and opened at song 1, Waiting (Reprise). A line on screen says "New setlist: GM Tribute — Cryer Arts".
- **Your own setlists are not touched.** The September setlist, the rehearsal list and any other stay as they are, each with its place. A restore point is saved first.
- **It happens once on each copy.** The home-screen app and Safari keep separate data, so each gets the setlist the first time it opens. After that the setlist is yours: go back to another, change it or delete it, and it stays that way.
- **No second copy.** A new install, or a device where a setlist already has this exact order (after "Restore the GM Tribute order"), gets nothing added.
- Charts, keys, cues, notes and GX-10 memories are unchanged.
