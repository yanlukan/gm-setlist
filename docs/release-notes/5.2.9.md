# PlayBook 5.2.9: Kissing a Fool, every bar read from the recording

The player said something was not right with this song, and that its chords and the order of its parts must be exact. Until now the chords came from the songbook (no. 37) and had not been read chord by chord from the recording. Now every one of the 84 bars has been, with the recording's pitch corrected first (it sits about halfway between the book's key and the band's, which makes an ordinary reading a semitone unsafe).

**The order of parts is right.** Intro (4 bars), Verse (25), Bridge (9), Verse (25), Middle 8 (11), Last verse (5), Outro. Each part starts where the chart says, bar for bar.

**79 of the 84 bars are as the book has them. Five places changed** (chords here in the book's key; the chart shows them a semitone lower, in the band's):

1. **Verse, bar 16** (both times round): the book's Bb13 has a third that nobody plays there. The keys play Ab, C, Eb and G over the bass's Bb, and the third only arrives with the Bb7(b9) after it. Now **Abmaj7/Bb**.
2. **Verse, bar 25** (the link bar): the chart had Fdim7/Eb, taken from the book's chord box. The book's chord *name* there is a plain diminished chord over Eb, and that is what is played: F, Ab and Cb over the held Eb, the same notes as the Abm6 of bar 12. Now **Abm6/Eb**. The old box had a note nobody plays and is gone.
3. **Bridge, bar 1**: Eb lasts two beats, then Cm7 and C7(b9) take a beat each. The chart had the three chords sharing the bar equally.
4. **Middle 8, bar 10**: the fourth first, then the third: **Bb7sus4, Bb**. The chart had a plain Bb for the whole bar. In bar 11 the band drops out and the chord rings.
5. **Outro**: the band slows down. The last Cb7 and Bb7 take a bar each (they shared one), the voice finishes alone, and the final chord comes in at about 4:26 and rings. The Outro is 6 bars, not 5.

**Listened to closely and left as the book has them:** the Db6 in the Middle 8, the C7 bars (a ninth is sometimes heard on top), the Abm in the verse's fifth line (sometimes with a seventh), the Cb6, the Intro's plain Fm, and the Bb7(b9) in the break bar.

The two new chords have shapes with the right bass note: Gmaj7/A as 5-x-5-4-7-x and Gm6/D as x-5-5-3-5-x (band's key). The book's A13 box is no longer shown, as that chord is no longer in the song.

Not done: nobody has played the chart against the record yet. The reading is from the separated instrument tracks (bass, piano, the rest), beat by beat.
