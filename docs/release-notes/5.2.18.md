# PlayBook 5.2.18: the final running order, and GX-10 hints for the plain sounds

Two things for the Cryer Arts Theatre concert on 2026-11-28.

## The final order

Songs 3 and 4 change places: song 3 is now Papa Was a Rolling Stone (the Killer / Papa medley) and song 4 is I'm Your Man. The other 19 songs stay where they were.

- **It shows up by itself.** The first time the app opens after this update, a setlist named "Cryer Arts — final order" is added and opened at song 1, Waiting (Reprise). A line on screen says "New setlist: Cryer Arts — final order". Nothing to tap.
- **Your own setlists are not touched.** The setlist "GM Tribute — Cryer Arts" with the order of 22 November stays as it is, beside the new one, with its place. A restore point is saved first.
- **Once on each copy.** The home-screen app and Safari keep separate data, so each gets the setlist the first time it opens.
- **No second copy** on a new install, or where a setlist already has this exact order.
