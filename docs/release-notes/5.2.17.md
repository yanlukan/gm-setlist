# PlayBook 5.2.17: Fastlove, the intro is 16 bars from the first sound

The chart showed a 12-bar intro and left the record's first four bars out. Counted on the recording, the verse comes in at bar 17, so the intro is 16 bars:

`| Fmaj7/A | Am11 | Fmaj7/A | Am11 |`
`| Dm9 | Am11 | Dm9 | Am11 | (x3)`

The first four bars are the keys alone, the same two chords over a low A with no bass. The bass comes in at bar 5, the drums and the guitar at bar 9, and the voice sings over bars 13 to 16. The rest of the song is unchanged.
