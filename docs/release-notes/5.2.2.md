# PlayBook 5.2.2: Roxanne with the chords the player hears

After 5.2.1 the player wrote: "roxane starts from Bm7, A, later Bm, F#m, Gmaj7 f#m7 etc. its different". The recording agrees with the player, not with the book, in both places.

- **The vamp is Bm7, A**, in the Intro and the Interlude. The book's B11 has a B in the bass; on the recording the bass plays A there, under A, C#, E and F#.
- **The verse's second bar is F#m**, in the Intro's pass and in both verses: Bm, F#m, Gmaj7, F#m7. The book prints D/A. On the recording the piano plays F#, A and C# over the bass's A.
- **Shapes for A and F#m** are the app's recommended ones; the book has no box for them. The book's D/A box goes with its chord; it was the last in D/A's list, so no other saved pick moves.
- The rest is as in 5.2.1: Em7, G/A, B11 in the verse, the chorus, Pre-Chorus, Outro and Ending from the book.

Measured on the player's recording, one instrument at a time (bass, piano, guitar and the rest), bar by bar.
