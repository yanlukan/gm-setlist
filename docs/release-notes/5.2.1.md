# PlayBook 5.2.1: Roxanne starts where the recording starts

The Roxanne chart was 8 bars ahead of the recording from the first verse to the interlude. The player found it "totally wrong".

The cause was the intro. The book starts at the verse, so the intro came from the recording, and it was written as 4 bars. The recording plays 12: the 4 bars of the Interlude, then the verse chords once with no voice, where the bass comes in. The singer starts after that, at about 0:38. To make the bars add up, the first chorus had been written three times, and 4 bars were left out as unclear.

- **Intro: 12 bars.** Bm7, B11, Bm7, B11, then Bm, D/A, Gmaj7, F#m7, Em7, G/A and B11 held two bars.
- **First chorus: twice, as in the book**, not three times. It ends B11, then N.C.
- **The verses, Pre-Chorus, second chorus, Outro and Ending are unchanged.** From the Interlude on, the chart already matched the recording.
- The note under the chart no longer speaks of 4 unclear bars.

Measured on the player's recording from the separated bass, piano and voice: bass note and harmony on every beat, and where the voice sings. The recording check was run again for Roxanne: it lines up better (0.51 to 0.56) and the three Verse 2 bars it could not place are placed.
