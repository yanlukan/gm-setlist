# PlayBook 5.0.0: the rework goes live

Replaces 4.2.8. Same address (https://yanlukan.github.io/gm-setlist/), same saved data: your edited charts, picks, setlists, own songs, own shapes and settings stay where they are on each copy (home-screen app and Safari). Take the backup in docs/sessions/switch-live.md first anyway.

What changed since 4.2.8 (built as 4.9.0 to 4.13.0 on branch `rework`):

- **Beats and layout (4.9.0).** Charts carry beat dots (`B  F#/A#  G#m  .`); bars sit in beat columns; one chart size, bigger chords on every song; crowded bars stagger above and below their beats.
- **Chart-first screens (4.10.0).** The Practice panel is gone: tap a chord to see its shapes in a sheet. Stage stays tap-free. One title line (key, BPM, time signature, capo or lower-key warnings, EDITED on Practice, the GX-10 sound on Stage). Key, capo, guitar, position, Edit, songbook and notes live in More, under "This song". Edit is now More, then "Edit the chart".
- **Where the chords came from (4.11.0).** Each song says whether its chords are from the book, a chart, the record or your ear. A bar nobody is sure of shows a small ?; on Practice a tap shows the readings and one tap settles it.
- **Checked against the recordings (4.12.0).** 11 bars in 5 songs are marked unsure where the record disagrees with the chart.
- **Adding any song (4.13.0).** A Mac command turns a recording, and a chart if there is one, into a song file; it joins the library and goes into a set with Edit list.
- **Switch-over (5.0.0).** Tests prove a 4.2.8 device's data opens unchanged and a 4.2.8 backup file loads into the new app.

Not checked yet on the real iPad: staggered bars (Faith, Fastlove, I Can't Make You Love Me, Papa, Wake Me Up), Edit taking two taps, the artist and Book badge gone from the title line.
